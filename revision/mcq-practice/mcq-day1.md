## MCQ 1
Which of the following is NOT a core component of "software" as defined in Lecture 1?
- A. Computer programs
- B. Procedures
- C. Hardware drivers
- D. Documentation

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The 4 core components are computer programs, procedures, documentation, and data needed to operate the system. Hardware drivers are not listed as a software core component.

---

## MCQ 2
A logic mistake that a programmer commits while coding (e.g., multiplying instead of adding) is best classified as a:
- A. Software failure
- B. Software fault
- C. Software error
- D. Software risk

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** An error is made by the programmer (syntax or logic). It only becomes a fault if it lands in code, and a failure only when the fault is activated.

---

## MCQ 3
According to Lecture 1, how many distinct causes of software errors are identified?
- A. 5
- B. 6
- C. 7
- D. 8

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** 8 causes: faulty requirements, communication failures, deliberate deviations, logical design errors, coding errors, non-compliance with instructions, testing shortcomings, procedure/documentation errors.

## MCQ 4
Whose definition states that software quality is "Conformance to explicit requirements, documented standards, and implicit characteristics"?
- A. Crosby (1979)
- B. Juran
- C. Pressman (2000)
- D. McCall (1977)

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** This is Pressman's 2000 definition, which combines explicit requirements, documented standards, and implicit GSEP characteristics.

## MCQ 5
Which best contrasts SQA with SQC?
- A. SQA evaluates the developed product; SQC evaluates the process.
- B. SQA evaluates the process; SQC evaluates the developed product.
- C. SQA and SQC are identical activities.
- D. SQC includes SQA as a sub-activity.

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Lecture 1 page 23–24: SQC is a set of activities to evaluate the quality of a developed/manufactured product; SQA evaluates the process by which products are developed. QC is a part of QA.

---

## MCQ 6
Which standard is classified as a Quality Management Standard (focus "WHAT")?
- A. IEEE 1012
- B. ISO/IEC 12207
- C. ISO 9001
- D. None of the above

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** ISO 9001, ISO 9000-3, and SEI CMM are management standards. IEEE 1012 and ISO/IEC 12207 are project process standards.

---

## MCQ 7
McCall's quality model classifies factors into how many categories?
- A. 2
- B. 3
- C. 5
- D. 11

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** 3 categories — Product Operation, Product Revision, Product Transition — containing 11 factors in total.

## MCQ 8
Which factor is NOT a McCall Product Operation factor?
- A. Correctness
- B. Reliability
- C. Maintainability
- D. Integrity

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Maintainability belongs to Product Revision. Product Operation factors are Correctness, Reliability, Efficiency, Integrity, Usability.

## MCQ 9
Which group does Portability belong to in McCall's model?
- A. Product Operation
- B. Product Revision
- C. Product Transition
- D. Product Sustainability

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Product Transition factors are Portability, Reusability, Interoperability.

## MCQ 10
The Deutsch & Willis quality model has:
- A. 11 factors / 3 categories
- B. 12 factors / 3 categories
- C. 15 factors / 4 categories
- D. 15 factors / 3 categories

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Deutsch & Willis (1988) = 15 factors in 4 categories. Evans & Marciniak (1987) = 12 factors / 3 categories.

## MCQ 11
Which factor is unique to the Deutsch & Willis model (not in Evans & Marciniak)?
- A. Verifiability
- B. Expandability
- C. Safety
- D. Reusability

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Safety and Manageability are unique to Deutsch & Willis. Verifiability and Expandability are shared by both alternative models.

## MCQ 12
Which McCall factor is essentially equivalent to "Survivability" in alternative models?
- A. Efficiency
- B. Reliability
- C. Flexibility
- D. Testability

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Survivability concerns continuity of service, akin to McCall's Reliability.

Which factor is excluded from BOTH Evans–Marciniak and Deutsch–Willis models?

- C. Usability

## MCQ 17
Total Quality Cost is composed of:
- A. Costs of Prevention + Costs of Appraisal
- B. Costs of Control + Costs of Failure
- C. Internal cost + External cost
- D. Development cost + Maintenance cost

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Total Quality Cost = Costs of Control (Prevention + Appraisal + Managerial control) + Costs of Failure (Internal + External + Managerial).

## MCQ 18
A Formal Design Review (DR) committee typically includes:
- A. Peers of the developer only
- B. Outside experts only
- C. Senior professionals including the project leader
- D. The project's testers exclusively

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** DR committees are composed of senior professionals including the project leader. Peer reviews use peers as reviewers.

## MCQ 19
Which contrast best describes Procedures vs. Work Instructions?
- A. Procedures are specific; work instructions are general
- B. Procedures are general for the organization; work instructions are highly detailed for specialized teams
- C. Both are identical concepts
- D. Procedures apply only to testing; work instructions only to coding

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Procedures = general definitions for the organization. Work instructions = detailed directions for specific methods used by specialized teams.

## MCQ 25
Which property is NOT part of the F.I.R.S.T. principle for good unit tests?
- A. Fast
- B. Isolated
- C. Repeatable
- D. Manual

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** F.I.R.S.T = Fast, Isolated, Repeatable, Self-Validating, Timely. Tests should be self-validating (pass/fail automatic), never manual.
